# SlotWise design system

A calm, mobile-first booking workspace for independent studios. Customer and owner areas share the same compact shell.

- Colors: background #f5f6f2; surface #ffffff; border #dce2dc; text #172b22; muted #53675d; accent #22694c; success #28734d; danger #a73636.
- Type: system sans; 15px body, 20px section headings, 32px page headings; tabular numbers for dates, prices and times.
- Spacing: 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32px. Radii: 10px and 16px.
- Components: responsive top navigation, cards, forms, buttons, notices, calendar controls and booking lists. Touch targets are at least 44px.
