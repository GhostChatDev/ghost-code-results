Build a responsive, installable web app called "SlotWise" for small studios (hair, fitness, tutoring)
to take bookings.

STACK
- Next.js (App Router) + TypeScript + Tailwind CSS
- Postgres through Prisma (DATABASE_URL), with migrations
- Email/password sign-in with secure, httpOnly session cookies and hashed passwords

ROLES
- Owner: sets up the studio, services and opening hours, sees every booking.
- Customer: signs up, books, reschedules and cancels their own bookings only.

SCREENS (mobile first, big tap targets)
1. Public studio page: services (name, duration, price) and a "Book" button.
2. Booking: pick a service, then a day on a week calendar, then a free time slot.
3. My bookings (customer): upcoming and past, with Reschedule and Cancel.
4. Owner dashboard: today's and this week's bookings, with customer name and service.
5. Owner settings: services, opening hours per weekday, breaks, and days off.

RULES
1. No double booking: two customers can never get overlapping slots, even if they
   book at the same moment (enforce it in the database, not only in the UI).
2. Slots come from opening hours minus breaks, days off and existing bookings,
   in the studio's time zone. Store times in UTC.
3. Customers cancel or reschedule up to 2 hours before the start; after that,
   only the owner can.
4. A customer never sees another customer's name, email or bookings.
5. Rate-limit sign-in and booking.

TESTS (write and run them)
- Double booking is rejected, including two bookings at the same time.
- Slots respect hours, breaks, days off and existing bookings across a DST change.
- The 2-hour cancel rule.
- Customer A cannot read, change or cancel customer B's bookings.
