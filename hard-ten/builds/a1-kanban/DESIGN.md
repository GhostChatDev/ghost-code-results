# Corkline design system

Corkline is a shared team board, styled like a calm, tactile workspace rather than a dense spreadsheet. The demo is shared and has no sign-in; do not use it for private information without adding access control.

- Background `#f5f3ed`, surface `#ffffff`, border `#e4e3db`, text `#252d29`, muted `#64716a`, accent `#286c54`, success `#23744f`, danger `#a23737`.
- Typography: system sans-serif; 14px body, 12px captions, 20px section titles, 30px page title; tabular numerals for counts and dates.
- Spacing: 4, 8, 12, 16, 24 and 32px; 8px and 14px radii.
- Components: workspace header, board heading, sync indicator, history buttons, horizontally scrolling columns, draggable cards, labels, card editor, checklist and status messages.
- On narrow screens columns remain individually scrollable horizontally, with controls wrapping and touch-friendly drag handles.
