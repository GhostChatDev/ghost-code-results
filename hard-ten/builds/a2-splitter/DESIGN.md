# Evenstep design

Evenstep is a practical, calm ledger for people sharing costs. The app is shared by anyone who can access its deployment; it does not implement accounts or access control. Deploy behind an access-controlled network if the data is private.

- Colours: background #f5f6f2, surface #ffffff, border #e0e5df, text #172c24, muted #576b61, accent #236b4e, success #216b4c, danger #a23737.
- Type: system sans-serif; body 15px, small 13px, headings 24px / 32px / 40px. Money uses tabular numbers.
- Spacing: 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32px. Radii: 8px and 16px.
- Components: sidebar navigation, group selector, summary cards, balance rows, expense and payment forms, activity list, CSV link, notices and empty states.
- Layout: desktop sidebar and content grid; stacked navigation and cards on small screens. Labels and controls remain visible and keyboard accessible.
- Amounts and rates are user-entered, not simulated.
